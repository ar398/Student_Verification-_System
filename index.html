Frequently Asked Questions

How long does verification take?
Once you upload your documents, institutions typically verify them within 24 to 48 hours depending on their workload. You can track the status live on your dashboard.
Is my document data secure?
Yes, absolutely. All documents are stored securely and are only accessible by you, the targeted institution, and state administrators.
What if my request is rejected?
If rejected, the institution will leave a remark explaining why (e.g., blurred ID). You can review the remark, correct the issue, and submit a new request instantly.
Can I apply for multiple scholarships across states?
Yes! ScholarVerify is designed specifically for cross-state verification. You can submit your verified profile to any integrated state board seamlessly.
